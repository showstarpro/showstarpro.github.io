I am a final-year Ph.D. student at HUST, advised by Prof. Kun He. I study how to learn visual representations that transfer across vision-language systems: image-text pretraining, CLIP post-training, self-supervised visual pretraining, and vision encoders for multimodal large language models. Since 2024 I have also been a research intern at Li Auto, working on large-scale training of vision and vision-language models.

Selected

  • NeurIPS 2026
    ITO: Multi-View Alignment and Training-Time Fusion for Image-Text Pretraining
    Hanpeng Liu, Zidan Wang, Shuoxi Zhang, Zonglin Zhao, Zihao Bo, Rinyoichi Takezoe, Kaiwen Long, Yaqian Li, Kun He
    Aligns images and texts across multiple views and adds a fusion module that is used only during training, so inference keeps the cost of a standard dual encoder.
    Paper Code
  • NeurIPS 2026
    Rethinking Contrastive Loss in CLIP Post-training: A Complementary Framework with Frozen Text Encoder
    Zidan Wang, Yaqian Li, Xiaokai Zhang, Kaiwen Long, Kun He, Hanpeng Liu†
    Shows that forgetting in CLIP post-training is driven by the InfoNCE temperature rather than small batches, and keeps the text encoder frozen so the improved vision encoder drops into LLaVA without re-alignment.
    Paper Code Project page
  • Preprint
    iGVLM: Dynamic Instruction-Guided Vision Encoding for Question-Aware Multimodal Understanding
    Hanpeng Liu, Yaqian Li, Zidan Wang, Shuoxi Zhang, Zihao Bo, Rinyoichi Takezoe, Kaiwen Long, Kun He
    Conditions the vision encoder of a multimodal LLM on the user's question, so the visual features adapt to each instruction.
    Paper
  • CVPR 2024
    You Only Need Less Attention at Each Stage in Vision Transformers
    Shuoxi Zhang*, Hanpeng Liu*, Stephen Lin, Kun He
    An efficient vision transformer that computes self-attention in only a few layers of each stage and reuses it in the rest.
    Paper
